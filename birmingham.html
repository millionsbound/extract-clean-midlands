Kitchen extract cleaning in Birmingham

Enquiries from restaurants, takeaways and other commercial kitchens in Birmingham and nearby parts of the West Midlands.

If you run a kitchen in Birmingham, Solihull, Sutton Coldfield or another nearby part of the West Midlands, you can request a canopy, extract or grease trap clean through this site.

We check the postcode, kitchen type, the work needed, when the system was last cleaned and how urgent it is, then pass the enquiry to one specialist firm that covers the area. That firm contacts you to quote.

Birmingham has a large number of restaurants and takeaways, from the city centre and the Jewellery Quarter to neighbourhood high streets. The same fire safety duties apply in a small takeaway as in a larger restaurant: grease in the extract system is a hazard, and it belongs in your fire risk assessment.

If you have been asked for a TR19-style certificate by a landlord or insurer, mention that on the form so the firm can quote for a documented clean.
