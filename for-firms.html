For cleaning firms

We collect enquiries from restaurants and takeaways that need extract, duct or grease trap cleaning, check them, and pass each one to a single firm in that area.

What we pass you

This is a lead referral arrangement, not a customer enquiry for our own kitchen, and not a job we will carry out ourselves.

Every enquiry is checked before it is sent on. We collect and review:

  • the postcode, so it is actually in your area
  • the type of kitchen
  • the work they say they need
  • when the system was last cleaned, if they know
  • how urgent it is
  • a name, phone number and email so you can call them back

If the details are incomplete or the job is outside the area, we do not pass it to you as a live enquiry.

One firm per area

We appoint one firm to cover a given area. Checked enquiries from that area go to that firm, not to a pool of companies bidding on the same job.

You quote the customer, carry out the work if they go ahead, and they pay you. We do not set your prices, and we do not take payment from the restaurant.

What we look for in a firm

We want firms that already clean commercial kitchen extracts to a recognised standard, such as TR19 Grease, and that will provide a proper post-clean record. We will talk this through with you before any enquiries are sent.

Talk to us about covering an area

If you clean kitchen extracts to a recognised standard and want to be considered for one area, send your details. We will only be in touch about this referral arrangement.
