Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data we collect when you contact us, why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights under UK data protection law.

Who we are

The controller is Extract Clean Midlands. Contact us via the enquiry form or at hamzatsvj@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • the business name and postcode
  • the kitchen type, last clean date or rough timing, the work needed and how urgent it is
  • anything else you choose to tell us in the message box
  • if you are a cleaning firm, the firm name and the areas you cover

We also collect basic technical data needed to run the website securely, such as an IP address in server logs. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

PurposeLawful basis
Checking your enquiry and contacting you about itSteps at your request before a contract, and legitimate interests in responding
Sharing a restaurant or takeaway enquiry with one specialist cleaning firm that covers your postcodeYour consent
Talking to a cleaning firm about covering an areaLegitimate interests in arranging referrals, and steps at your request
Keeping a record of enquiries and consentLegal obligation and legitimate interests
MarketingOnly with separate, specific consent. We do not do this by default

Who we share it with

  • One specialist cleaning firm that covers your postcode, after you have agreed to this on the form
  • Service providers under contract, including the form delivery service (FormSubmit) and the email and hosting providers used to run this service
  • Authorities if the law requires it

We do not sell your data. We do not pass the same enquiry to several firms.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and consent records are kept for 2 years after last contact, then deleted, unless we must keep them longer to deal with a complaint or a legal claim.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, restrict or object, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Contact us via the enquiry form or at hamzatsvj@gmail.com. You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Extract CleanMidlands

An enquiry service for restaurants and takeaways that need kitchen extract, duct or grease trap cleaning across the West Midlands, the Black Country and the East Midlands, including Nottingham.

We check each enquiry and pass it to one specialist firm that covers your area. That firm does the work and quotes you directly.
