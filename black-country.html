Kitchen extract cleaning in Black Country

Enquiries from commercial kitchens in Dudley, Wolverhampton, Walsall, Sandwell and the towns around them.

The Black Country has a dense mix of takeaways, pubs, restaurants and works canteens. If your kitchen is in Dudley, Wolverhampton, Walsall, West Bromwich, Sandwell or a nearby town, you can send an extract cleaning enquiry here.

We check the details and pass the enquiry to one specialist firm that covers the Black Country. The firm quotes you directly and carries out the work.

Opening hours in the area are often long, especially for takeaways. Under BESA's published TR19 guidance, a kitchen running 12 to 16 hours a day is in the "heavy use" band, which has a minimum clean every three months, unless your fire risk assessment or insurer sets a different interval.
