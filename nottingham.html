Kitchen extract cleaning in Nottingham

Enquiries from commercial kitchens in Nottingham and nearby parts of Nottinghamshire, in the East Midlands.

Extract Clean Midlands also takes enquiries from the East Midlands, starting with Nottingham and the towns around it. If you run a restaurant, takeaway, cafe, pub or other commercial kitchen here, you can request a clean through the same form.

We check the postcode and the job details, then pass the enquiry to one specialist firm that covers Nottingham and the surrounding area. That firm quotes you directly.

The city centre, Hockley, West Bridgford, Beeston and neighbourhood takeaway parades all sit under the same fire safety rules. A documented extract clean is often what a landlord or insurer wants to see.
