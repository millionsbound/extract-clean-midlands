What we help with

Extract systems, canopies and grease traps

Commercial kitchens need regular cleaning of the canopy, filters, ductwork and fans. Grease that builds up inside the extract system is a fire hazard, and filters alone do not catch all of it.

TR19 Grease, published by the Building Engineering Services Association (BESA), is the recognised UK specification for managing that fire risk. Many insurers also ask for proof that the system has been cleaned to a recognised standard.

Read what TR19 covers or why cleaning matters for fire and insurance.
