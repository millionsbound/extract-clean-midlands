Extract CleanMidlands

An enquiry service for restaurants and takeaways that need kitchen extract, duct or grease trap cleaning across the West Midlands, the Black Country and the East Midlands, including Nottingham.

We check each enquiry and pass it to one specialist firm that covers your area. That firm does the work and quotes you directly.
