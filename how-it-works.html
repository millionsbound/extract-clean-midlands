How it works for restaurants

You send one enquiry. We check it. One specialist firm in your area comes back to you with a quote.

1

Fill in the enquiry form

We ask for the business name, your name, phone, email and postcode, plus the kitchen type, when the system was last cleaned, the work you need and how urgent it is. The more accurate this is, the quicker a firm can quote.

2

We check the enquiry

We review the details so that what is passed on is a real kitchen in an area we cover, with enough information for a specialist firm to understand the job. If something important is missing, we may come back to you first.

3

One firm in your area is given the enquiry

We do not auction your details to several companies. One firm that covers your postcode receives the checked enquiry.

4

That firm contacts you

They will quote, agree access and book the visit. They carry out the clean and should give you the post-clean records. You pay the firm, not us.

What to expect

Clear about who does what

Extract Clean Midlands is an enquiry service. We are not the cleaning contractor, and we do not send a team of our own. The specialist firm you are introduced to is responsible for the standard of the work, the price, and the certificate or report they issue.

If you are not happy with a quote, you do not have to go ahead. If you would rather we did not share your details, say so and we will stop.

Cleaning firms can read more about the arrangement on the page for cleaning firms.
