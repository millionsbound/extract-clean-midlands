Kitchen extract cleaning in Coventry

Enquiries from restaurants, takeaways and other commercial kitchens in Coventry and the surrounding towns.

If you run a kitchen in Coventry or a nearby town, you can request a canopy, duct or grease trap clean through this site. We check the enquiry and pass it to one specialist firm that covers the Coventry area.

The firm will contact you to quote, agree access and, if you go ahead, carry out the clean and provide the post-clean records.

Whether you are in the city centre, around the universities, or on a local parade, the legal duty is the same. The responsible person must assess fire risks, and grease in the extract system is one of those risks.
